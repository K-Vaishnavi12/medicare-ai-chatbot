How do I choose a specialist?

General information. A general medicine clinician is often a good first contact. They can assess your needs and suggest an appropriate specialty when necessary.

What should I ask about a medicine?

General information. Ask your clinician or pharmacist about the intended use, dosing, possible side effects, interactions, and what to do if you miss a dose. Share any known allergies with them.

When should I seek urgent help?

General information. If you think someone has a medical emergency, seek immediate local emergency assistance. Do not rely on an online information tool for urgent decisions.
