How a real digital prescription would work

Doctors create, edit, and approve digital prescriptions after clinical review. Patients can view, download, or print approved prescriptions. QR-code verification can support prescription checking. Medicine reminders and prescription history support follow-up. AI must not independently issue prescriptions. All prescriptions require review and approval by a qualified doctor.
