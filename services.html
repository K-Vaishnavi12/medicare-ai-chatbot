How AI improves the solution

Artificial intelligence acts as a support layer. It can interpret user-entered symptoms to provide general health guidance, answer common healthcare questions, and suggest an appropriate medical specialty based on the information supplied. For doctors, AI can help organize clinical notes and summarize relevant information for review.

  • A symptom checker that offers general guidance and encourages appropriate medical consultation
  • A healthcare chatbot for common healthcare-related questions
  • Suggestions for potentially relevant medical specialists
  • Assistance organizing clinical notes and patient information
  • Alerts for potential medicine allergies and drug interactions when reliable reference data is available
  • Multilingual access in English, Hindi, and Telugu

Responsible AI safeguard: AI must not independently diagnose patients or issue prescriptions. It must not replace a doctor’s judgment. Doctors review and approve all prescriptions, and users should be directed to qualified healthcare professionals for medical decisions or urgent concerns.

Dashboards

  • Patient dashboard: profile and medical history, appointments, prescriptions, reminders, symptom checker, and chatbot.
  • Doctor dashboard: patient information, appointment management, clinical notes, and prescription drafting and approval.
  • Admin dashboard: patient and doctor management, doctor verification, appointment and prescription monitoring, and platform statistics.
  • Prescription module: structured form, doctor signature, PDF download and print, and QR-code verification.

Technology

The proposed technology layer is React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Supabase, and a securely integrated AI API.
