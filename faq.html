FAQ

Questions about this website

What is MediCare AI?

MediCare AI is a proposed AI-powered healthcare and digital prescription system. This public website explains that concept and includes a chatbot. It is an informational demonstration, not a clinic.

What does the home page say?

The headline is “Smart Healthcare, Simplified with AI.” It invites people to explore specialists and trusted general guidance, and it states that healthcare decisions stay with qualified professionals.

Can the chatbot diagnose me?

No. AI must not independently diagnose patients or issue prescriptions. Answers are general information and cannot replace professional care.

Who are the sample doctors?

Dr. Ananya Rao (general medicine), Dr. Vikram Mehta (cardiology), and Dr. Sahana Iyer (dermatology). They are illustrative profiles only.

Is the sample prescription valid?

No. It is labeled SAMPLE, the reference says DEMO ONLY · NOT VERIFIABLE, the signature is not signed, and no QR verification is available.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi, and Telugu.

Does this website store my health details?

No. The guidance section says no symptoms or personal details are collected. Do not type private medical information into the chatbot.

What should I do in an emergency?

Contact local emergency services immediately. Do not rely on this website for urgent decisions.
