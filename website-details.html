About

Title: Made for better questions. MediCare AI is a concept for more accessible care. This demonstration helps you explore specialties and understand how a clinician-reviewed care journey could look. It does not connect to a clinic or store patient records.

The problem: appointments, medical history, prescriptions, and follow-up instructions are often split across channels. Patients may struggle to find doctors, track health information, understand instructions, or access prescription records. MediCare AI proposes one digital platform for doctor connection, appointments, digital prescriptions, and general AI-assisted guidance, with clinical decisions kept under professional control.

Primary users: patients, doctors, caregivers or family members, and people seeking general health information. Secondary users: pharmacists, clinic and hospital administrators, and healthcare support staff.

Scenarios: search doctors by specialization; book, view, or cancel appointments; review patient profiles and history; create, edit, and approve prescriptions after clinical review; view, download, or print prescriptions; medicine reminders and history; symptom checker or chatbot for general information; QR-code verification of prescription details. It does not replace an in-person assessment.

Impact: easier doctor search and appointments, access to digital prescriptions and records, reminders and follow-up, less paperwork, organized clinician workflows, general health information, allergy and interaction awareness, and English, Hindi, and Telugu access.

Difference from traditional care: one experience with patient, doctor, and admin dashboards; registration and profiles; specialization search; doctor-approved prescriptions with PDF and QR; bounded AI; reminders; multilingual responsive design.

Testing status: the brief does not show clinical validation or organizational deployment. Sample data must be labeled as demonstration data.

Services

Kicker: What you can explore. Title: Care that feels less complicated. Explore specialists: browse fictional sample clinicians by area of practice. General guidance: plain-language answers to care-navigation questions without private health information. Clinician-led decisions: a sample document layout; real prescriptions need a licensed clinician.

AI support includes general symptom guidance, a healthcare chatbot, specialty suggestions, help organizing clinical notes, allergy and interaction alerts when reliable data exists, and multilingual access. AI must not diagnose or prescribe.

Modules: patient dashboard, doctor dashboard, admin dashboard, and prescription module. Technology: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Supabase, and a secure AI API.

Data the platform may handle

Patient profiles and contact information, medical history, symptoms and notes, doctor profiles and availability, prescription details, allergy and interaction reference data, appointments, reminders, and role-based account records. Handling should use permissions, role-based access, secure authentication, protected storage, and data minimization. This public site does not store those records.

Challenges

Protecting sensitive information, separate role workflows, doctor approval of prescriptions, responsible AI wording, reliable interaction data, printable PDFs with QR codes, consistent records, three languages, and an accessible responsive interface.
